# Preguntas para los comerciantes de registro

Volver al [[00 - Indice]] · Contexto: [[04 - Fase 2 - Fabrica de Micro-Apps]] (sección 6)

Las investigaciones no pudieron confirmar si estas plataformas pagan a México, a quién (persona física o moral) y si aceptan las categorías de nuestros productos. Hay que **preguntarlo por escrito** y guardar las respuestas como evidencia de la política vigente.

## A quién enviarlo
Creem, Polar, Paddle y Lemon Squeezy; Gumroad como cuarta opción. Usa el formulario o el correo de soporte oficial de cada una, desde un correo tuyo, y **no adjuntes documentos personales** en esta primera consulta.

## Mensaje (en inglés, que es el idioma habitual del soporte)

> **Subject:** Seller in Mexico — payouts, entity type and product category
>
> Hi,
>
> I plan to sell desktop software licenses (one-time purchase with 12 months of updates, between $19 and $49 USD) through [PLATFORM] as the merchant of record. Before building the products I would like to confirm a few things:
>
> 1. **Payouts to Mexico.** Can you pay a seller based in Mexico? First as an individual (persona física with a Mexican tax ID, RFC) and later as a company (persona moral)? In which currency does the money arrive, to what kind of account (for example a Mexican bank account/CLABE or PayPal), and what are the payout fees and minimums?
> 2. **Identity verification.** Which documents do you require for an individual and for a Mexican company, and how long does approval usually take?
> 3. **Changing entity.** If I start as an individual and later move to a company, can I convert the same account, or must I open a new one? What happens to my products, customers and license keys?
> 4. **Product categories.** Are these products allowed? All of them run entirely on the customer's own computer and none uploads any data:
>    (a) a Windows tool that records the user's own clicks and screenshots to create step-by-step guides, with automatic masking of sensitive data;
>    (b) a tool that helps users mask names and phone numbers in their own interview audio recordings, with mandatory human review;
>    (c) a tool that converts bank statement PDFs that the user owns into spreadsheets;
>    (d) a tool that splits several scanned photos into individual files.
> 5. **License keys.** Do your license keys support activation limits and offline validation?
> 6. **Tax documents.** What tax forms or invoices do you need from a seller in Mexico? (I will confirm the details with my accountant.)
>
> Thank you.
> [Your name]

## Para tu contador

Estas tres cosas no las pude averiguar y son de su competencia:
1. Qué documento fiscal (factura, CFDI o formulario W-8BEN) pide cada comerciante de registro.
2. Si el comerciante cuenta como cliente extranjero.
3. Cómo se trata el paso de persona física a persona moral, y si conviene empezar directamente con la persona moral que ya existe.
